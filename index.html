SHOM Current Viewer

Choose an atlas from the list, or open a local SHOM NetCDF file with eastward and northward current components.

Estimate from SHOM atlas data, blended by tide coefficient. Not suitable for navigation on its own. Use with official charts and nautical publications.

Currents: © Shom, Licence Ouverte 2.0 (file of ). Tides: api-maree.fr, CC BY.

Tide data

Tide times and heights come from api-maree.fr through this site's server.

Offline: enter the reference tide by hand.

Changes made to the data

            

              The atlas files are converted into a compact binary format for fast loading. Between the two published cases
              (tide coefficients 45 and 95) the arrows are interpolated linearly to the coefficient of the tide.
              Outside 45 to 95 the values are extrapolated (coefficients are limited to 20 to 120), which is less reliable.
              The time axis is aligned to the high or low water of the reference port, taken from api-maree.fr.
            

            

              The speeds and directions shown are therefore derived estimates, not the values published by Shom.
              Shom is not responsible for any modification made to the data it distributes.
              This application is independent and is not endorsed by Shom, Ifremer or api-maree.fr.

Privacy

            
    
              
  • 
                Your settings (arrow detail, time zone) and cached tide data are stored in your browser.
                The app works offline with the atlas data and map tiles it has already loaded.
              
    • 
              
  • 
                Tide requests go to this site's server, which forwards them to api-maree.fr and keeps the answers.
                To enforce usage limits, the server temporarily keeps a hashed form of your IP address for a few days.
                This app uses no cookies and no analytics. The web server may log requests as usual.
              
    • 
              
  • 
                Map tiles are loaded directly from the OpenStreetMap and OpenSeaMap servers,
                which therefore see your IP address.
